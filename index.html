How to use LPN Assistant

  1. Enter your hourly rate in Pay.
  2. Enter or import your WFM schedule in Calendar.
  3. Tap a shift to record swaps or changes.
  4. Calendar tracks hours; Pay converts those hours into an estimate.
  5. Home compares your actual shifts with your target.
  6. Keep your benefit-bank balances current in Banks.
  7. Use Pay Rules & Resources when you need the official rule.
